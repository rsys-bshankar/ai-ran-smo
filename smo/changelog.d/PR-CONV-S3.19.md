- **TE&IV serves the topology through the standards (plan tasks S3.19, S3.20, S3.21 in part).** The new module `teiv/` answers TS 28.532 ProvMnS getMOIAttributes of 15 TS 28.541 / TS 28.622 classes (SubNetwork, ManagedElement, GNBDUFunction, GNBCUCPFunction, NRCellDU, NRCellCU, NRSectorCarrier, NRCellRelation, NRFreqRelation, CommonBeamformingFunction and the SON functions) from O-RAN-SC smo-teiv, with scope, filter, attribute selection and paging, the TS 28.541 attributes (smo-teiv's columns and TE&IV's decorator schema `smo-teiv-attrs`, loaded at start-up) and the object version as `ETag`; it registers its classes in SME's MnS registry under `TEIV_MNS_SCOPE` (default `SubNetwork=SMO-RAN`) so R1 Termination routes them by class name. It also serves the 16 operations of the O-RAN WG10 TE&IV R1 API, passed to smo-teiv. Writes answer their checks (a stale `If-Match` is 412) and then 501 until the write path. New settings: `TEIV_EXPOSURE_URL`, `TEIV_CONNECT_TIMEOUT_SECONDS`, `TEIV_READ_TIMEOUT_SECONDS`, `TEIV_MAX_CONNECTIONS`, `TEIV_MAX_SCOPE_OBJECTS`, `TEIV_MNS_SCOPE` (`docs/configuration/teiv.md`). After a bulk load of smo-teiv, run `REINDEX SCHEMA teiv_data; ANALYZE;` in its database (`teiv/README.md`, "Operating"). TE&IV's service in compose and the chart follows in its own change.
